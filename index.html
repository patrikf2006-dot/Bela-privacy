Privacy Policy

Bela Rezultati Effective: October 2026 Last updated: October 2026

Short version: The developer does not collect your game data, and the app has no accounts or analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. From version 1.1.0 the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier and similar data to deliver and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Who we are

Bela Rezultati is developed and published by Patrik Ferenčak. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. Your game data stays on your device

The app does not collect, transmit, or share the information you enter. Scores, team names, player names, game titles, and your settings are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to any server, because the app has no server, no account system, and no login.

3. Advertising (version 1.1.0 and later)

Versions of the app before 1.1.0 contained no ads. From version 1.1.0 the app shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads. These are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom).

When an ad is requested, the AdMob software in the app, and Google, may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking, and the Android Advertising ID on Android)
  • Your IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and any errors

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, only where you have allowed it, to personalise ads. The developer does not receive this data in personal form. We receive only aggregated reports from Google, such as the number of ads shown and estimated earnings by country.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More detail on how Google uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Your choices

  • Consent form (EEA, UK, and Switzerland). When you first open the app, you will see a consent form where required by law. You can choose Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, in the item called Ad Privacy Settings.
  • iPhone and iPad. The app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the app does not access your advertising identifier. You can change this under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings under Ads. The exact location varies by device.
  • Google ad settings. You can manage personalised ads on your Google account at adssettings.google.com.

If you decline consent, you may see ads that are less relevant to you, or fewer ads.

5. Legal basis (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, storing or accessing information on your device for advertising, and showing personalised ads, is based on your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time using the choices in section 4. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

6. Your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Deleting the app removes all of your game data from your device.

For data that Google processes for advertising, you can use Google's own tools and request process described in its privacy policy, linked above. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Bela Rezultati is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob under Google's own policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date. The October 2026 update describes the advertising introduced in version 1.1.0.
