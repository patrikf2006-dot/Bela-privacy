Privacy Policy

Bela Rezultati Effective: September 2026 Last updated: September 2026

Short version: Bela Rezultati collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your own device and never leaves it.

1. Who we are

Bela Rezultati is developed and published by Patrik Ferenčak. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

All game data you enter — scores, team names, player names, game titles, and your settings — is saved only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server, because the app has no server, no account system, and no login.

3. No tracking, ads, or third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. It does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other personal data on your device. Nothing about your usage is monitored or reported.

4. Your data and your rights

Because the app stores its data only on your device and shares it with no one, you are always in full control of it. You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app, and nothing remains anywhere else.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), rights such as access, correction, and erasure under the GDPR are satisfied automatically: there is no data held by us to request, correct, or delete.

5. Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
